Privacy Policy / গোপনীয়তা নীতি

RamyaChobi · Photography & Cinematography
Last updated: 18 October 2026

This notice describes data used by the RamyaChobi website, Facebook client login, gallery access, photo selection, booking and delivery tools.

Information used

Facebook Login requests basic profile and email access. Depending on your consent and Facebook account, authentication may provide an account identifier, name, profile picture and email address. Supabase processes the login and maintains the authentication record. The website stores session information in your browser so you can remain signed in. Your Facebook password is entered on Facebook, not collected by RamyaChobi.

When you use other studio tools, information may include client and event details, contact information, gallery photos and selections, delivery access, payment records and uploaded payment proofs. These depend on the features you use.

Purpose and access

Data supports sign-in, return to your chosen gallery, photography services, photo selection, delivery, payment verification and support. Facebook sign-in is not proof that you own an album; existing gallery links, access permissions and delivery restrictions still apply.

Face Search and camera

The current gallery Face Search compares visual image features in your browser using the reference photo you choose or capture and photos from the current album. Camera use requires your permission. This matching code does not upload the reference image to an external face-recognition service. Album image previews are loaded from their hosting services. Matching results can be inaccurate; do not use them to verify someone's identity.

Facebook follows and reviews

Following the Page and writing a review are optional. The review button opens Facebook, where you submit the review under Facebook's settings and policies. A website button does not automatically publish a Facebook review. Selected public Facebook posts may be embedded on the website; loading an embed contacts Facebook and may involve Facebook cookies or account information under Facebook's policies. Reviews submitted separately through studio delivery tools are website feedback, not automatically Facebook reviews.

Service providers

The website uses Vercel for hosting, Supabase for authentication and application data, Google Drive for connected media storage and delivery, and Meta/Facebook for Facebook login and optional embedded posts. These services process information needed for their respective functions. Public website images and gallery previews may also load from the image hosting locations used by the studio.

Storage and retention

Login sessions and saved gallery selections can be stored in browser storage. Media availability and retention depend on the gallery or delivery settings and studio arrangements. Logging out does not itself delete the account, studio records, backups or Facebook reviews. Ask the studio about retention for your specific booking or data.

Your choices and deletion requests

You may decline optional camera access, avoid Facebook follow/review links, log out, or remove the app connection in Facebook. To request access, correction or deletion of data held by RamyaChobi, follow our Data Deletion Instructions. Removing an app from Facebook does not itself delete existing studio records.

Contact

Contact the studio through the official RamyaChobi Facebook Page. Do not send passwords, access tokens or payment secrets. For questions about a gallery, identify the event privately rather than posting a secure gallery link publicly.
